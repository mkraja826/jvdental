# JV Dental International Platform

A production-focused digital platform for JV Dental & Implant Centre.

## Product surfaces

- International implant and dental-tourism website
- Patient authentication and onboarding
- Medical and dental intake
- Secure patient-to-clinic messaging
- Clinic information and dental education AI assistant
- International patient CRM
- Treatment planning and estimates
- Implant-focused inventory and traceability
- Staff roles, audit history, and operations reporting

## Design direction

**Quiet Clinical Luxury** — editorial public website, calm patient portal, precise operational clinic portal. The logo is intentionally represented as a text wordmark until the final identity is approved.

## Status

Initial platform foundation in progress.
